The field

Miracle League of the Triangle

A fully accessible, purpose-built field. It is the same field the Miracle League uses for its own games, which is why the league raises money for them.

Before you go

  • Games run Friday nights, 6:20 PM to about 9:45 PM.
  • Four games a night, roughly 50 minutes apart.
  • Bring water. Bats and balls are provided.
  • Treat the field with care. It is built for the Miracle League's own players.
