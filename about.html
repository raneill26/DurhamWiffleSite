About the league

Eight teams, Friday nights, one cause. Managed by Play NC, a 501(c)(3) non-profit based in Durham since 2018.

The Durham Wiffle Ball League is a recreational, co-ed, reduced-speed wiffle ball league based in Durham, North Carolina. The 2026 season features eight teams, with opening night on Friday, April 10.

Where the money goes

Proceeds benefit Miracle League of the Triangle, which gives children with disabilities the chance to play baseball on a purpose-built, fully accessible field. The league plays its own games on that field, so the money goes straight back into the ground everyone is standing on.

What players get

  • A league jersey, made by RDU Print Studio in Durham.
  • Durham Bulls tickets, in the good seats, for the winners of the end-of-season tournament.
  • A game on one of the best fields you will ever play on.
  • The good feeling of supporting a Durham non-profit.

How the season runs

A nine-game regular season followed by a two-week playoff tournament. Games are Friday nights from April to September, on every Friday the Durham Bulls do not have a home game. There is an All-Star break on July 10, with a youth and adult tournament the following day.

Full detail on format, lineups and rainouts is on the rules page.
