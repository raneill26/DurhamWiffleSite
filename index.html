Why the league exists

The wiffle ball is how we fund the field.

Proceeds go to Miracle League of the Triangle, which gives children with disabilities the chance to play baseball on a purpose-built accessible field. We play our games on that field.

Friday nights

Four games a night at 6:20, 7:10, 8:00 and 8:50, done before 9:45.

Pitch to hit

A 40 mph speed limit with a radar gun, so more balls get put in play.

Rain helps

Cancelled games cannot be rescheduled, so that money goes to charity instead.
