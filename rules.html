League info

Rules and format

Recreational, co-ed, adult wiffle ball. Reduced speed, four a side, and done before 9:45.

The Durham Wiffle Ball League is a recreational, co-ed, reduced-speed wiffle ball league in Durham, North Carolina. All players are 18 or over.

Slow-pitch, pitch-to-hit

  • Speed limit of 40 mph, enforced with a radar gun.
  • Slightly harder than soft toss, but mostly pitch-to-hit.
  • This puts more balls in play and gives the fielders more to do.

Teams and lineups

  • Rosters are 8 players. Both team and individual registrations are accepted.
  • Individuals are grouped with about seven others to form a Free Agent team.
  • Teams need at least 4 players available to take the field.
  • Defensive lineups are 4 players a side. Offensive lineups include everyone present.

Game length

  • Regular season games are 6 innings or 45 minutes, whichever comes first.
  • Nine regular season games, plus up to two playoff games.
  • Games start at 6:20, 7:10, 8:00 and 8:50 PM. The last game finishes before 9:45.

The full game rules are available as a download. Read the rulebook

Rainouts and cancellations

The league plays every Friday night that the Durham Bulls do not have a home game, from April through September. Because every available Friday is already scheduled, there is no room to reschedule.

Rainouts cannot be rescheduled. When a game is cancelled for weather, more of the registration money goes to charity instead.

The season

  • Opening night: Friday, April 10.
  • Game nights: April 10 and 24, May 15 and 29, June 12 and 26, July 24, August 7 and 21, September 4 and 18.
  • All-Star break: July 10, with a youth and adult tournament on Saturday, July 11.
