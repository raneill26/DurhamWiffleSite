League info

Where the money goes

Every season raises money for Miracle League of the Triangle.

Play NC runs this league as a 501(c)(3) non-profit. Registration fees cover the cost of putting games on, and what is left goes to Miracle League of the Triangle.

Why the Miracle League

The Miracle League gives children with disabilities the chance to play baseball on a purpose-built, fully accessible field. The wiffle league plays every one of its games on that same field in Durham, which is a large part of why it is the beneficiary.

Rained-out games mean more for charity. Cancelled games cannot be rescheduled, so the money that would have gone into running them goes to the Miracle League instead.

You do not have to play to give

Donations to Play NC are tax-deductible. Questions about giving? playncinc@gmail.com
